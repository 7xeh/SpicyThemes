# Getting started

[← Docs index](README.md)

## First run

1. Play a track and open the lyrics view.
2. Click the **palette icon** in the lyrics controls to toggle Spicy Themes on and off.
3. **Right-click the palette icon** to open the settings modal — or use **ST Settings** in the Spicetify profile menu.
4. Pick a preset, or jump into **Customize** and start tweaking. Every change previews live.

A condensed version of the same settings also appears under **Spotify Settings → Spicy Themes**, next to the Spicy Lyrics section.

Spicy Themes works in the full-page lyrics view, the sidebar lyrics view, Cinema mode, and Picture-in-Picture.

## The settings modal

The sidebar lists every Customize group (Text, Glow, Focus, Motion, Background, Player, Translation) followed by **Presets**, **Marketplace**, and **About**. The styling on/off switch sits at the top of the sidebar.

- **Customize**: each group has a filter box that matches labels, hints, and synonyms (typing "neon" finds the glow controls). Sub-settings only appear once their parent is on, so the panel never floods you.
- **Presets**: apply a built-in or custom preset, save your current look as a new one, or delete custom presets.
- **Marketplace**: browse community themes with live previews, search by name or author, sort by Newest, Popular, or Featured, and hit **Apply** to use one straight away. Nothing gets downloaded as a file.
- **About**: installed version, build hash, export/import, reset, update checks, the changelog, and links to the repo, Marketplace, and Theme Creator.

The top bar has a few extra tools:

- **Changes**: lists every setting that differs from the preset you started from, with a per-setting revert, **Revert all**, and **Save as preset**.
- **Peek** (eye icon, or hold <kbd>P</kbd>): fades the window out while held so you can see the lyrics you're styling.
- **Notifications** (bell): past updates, warnings, and errors, so you don't miss one.
- **Search everything** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>): jump to any setting, page, preset, marketplace theme, or action.

Changes that replace your work (applying or reverting a preset, deleting one, importing, resetting) show a notification with **Undo**.

The popups take their colours from your current theme, so they change with it.

## Updates

When a new version is out, a small notification appears in the bottom-left corner. **Details** opens a card with the version change and the changelog. If a song is playing, **Update after this song** waits for the track to end, reloads, and starts the music again. **Not now** lets you snooze for 4 hours, snooze until tomorrow, or skip that version entirely. Patch releases (same version, fixed build) work the same way.

After an update, **What's new** shows the highlights. When a highlight maps to a setting, its **Try it** button jumps straight to that setting.
